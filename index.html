To My Mother ❤️

A small corner of the internet made just for you
You gave me life, you gave me light,
Through every storm, you held me tight.

When I was lost, you showed the way,
Your love remains, it will not sway.

No gift I bring could match your heart,
For you, my world, my truest start.
If I could build a world anew,
I’d build it softly, made for you.

With every smile you gave to me,
I learned what unconditional love should be.
Thank you for every sacrifice,
For all the times you paid the price.

Happy Mother’s Day ❤️
I love you always.
